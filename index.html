    KNOW THE RISK

    Disaster Information

    Quick information about common disasters, their warning signs and the immediate action you should take.

    🌊

    Flood

    Warning: Heavy rainfall and rising water levels.

    Action: Move to higher ground and avoid floodwater.

    IT Role: Water sensors, weather monitoring and mobile alerts.

    🌪️

    Cyclone

    Warning: Severe weather alerts, strong winds and heavy rain.

    Action: Stay indoors and follow evacuation instructions.

    IT Role: Satellites, weather forecasting and early warnings.

    🌍

    Earthquake

    Warning: Earthquakes generally have little or no reliable advance warning.

    Action: Drop, Cover and Hold On.

    IT Role: Seismic sensors and emergency communication systems.

    🔥

    Fire

    Warning: Smoke, unusual heat, flames or fire alarms.

    Action: Evacuate safely and contact emergency services.

    IT Role: Smoke sensors, heat detectors and automatic alarms.

    ⛰️

    Landslide

    Warning: Ground cracks, falling rocks or unusual slope movement.

    Action: Move away from unstable slopes and affected areas.

    IT Role: Rainfall monitoring, sensors and GIS risk mapping.

    🌊

    Tsunami

    Warning: Strong coastal earthquake, unusual sea-level changes or official alerts.

    Action: Move immediately to higher ground and away from the coast.

    IT Role: Seismic monitoring, ocean sensors and coastal warning systems.
